AI Bible for Small Business is a decision tool for small-business owners evaluating AI software. The
companion book provides the method; the app runs that method on the owner's own tools.

What to try (no account or sign-in needed):
1. Open the Decisions tab and tap "Evaluate my first AI tool".
2. Enter any tool name and task, then open Step 1, the Five-Question Filter, and answer the questions.
   This step is free.
3. Steps 2 and 3 (whole-job cost worksheet and trial checklist) are part of the one-time in-app purchase
   "Full Book and Tools". Buy it from any locked step, from a locked chapter, or from Settings. Restore
   Purchases is in Settings.
4. After starting a trial, set a review date and turn on "Remind me that morning". The app schedules a
   local notification; nothing is sent to a server.
5. Change the status to Kept or Dropped and add a note. The decision history is recorded. Use Share to
   export a one-page summary.
6. The Read tab contains the book. Chapter 1 is free; each step's "Why?" button opens the chapter that
   explains it.

The app has no accounts, no backend, no analytics and no tracking. All records are stored on the device.
