## Summary
The app does not collect any personal data. It has no account, no server of its own, no analytics
and no advertising or tracking.

## What stays on your device
Your reading position, bookmarks, and tool records (Filter answers, cost worksheets, trial checklists,
decisions, notes and review dates) are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are
included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple or you control. We cannot see
them.

## Reminders
If you turn on a review reminder, the app schedules a notification on your iPhone. It is created and
delivered on the device; nothing is sent to us.

## Purchases
Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The
app checks with Apple whether you own the full book so it can unlock it.

## Sharing
Nothing is shared unless you use the Share button, which hands text you choose to the app or person
you pick.

## Deleting your data
Settings → Delete My Data removes everything the app stored. Deleting the app also removes it.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
