## Common questions
**How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?** Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases,
signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

**I bought the ebook elsewhere. Does it unlock the app?** No. The app is a separate product with its
own tools; Apple's rules don't allow codes from other stores to unlock it.

**Where is my data?** Only on your iPhone (and its backups). There is no account or cloud sync.

**How do I delete my data?** Settings → Delete My Data.

**I'm not getting review reminders.** Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → AI Bible, and that
"Remind me that morning" is on for that tool.

**Refunds** are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
